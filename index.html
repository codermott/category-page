Frequently Asked Questions

What type of fishing reel is best for beginners?

Spincast reels are the easiest starting point — simple to use and forgiving of errors. Spinning reels offer better distance and versatility. Baitcast reels deliver the most precision and power but have a steeper learning curve.

What fishing line should I use?

Monofilament is affordable and forgiving — great for beginners. Fluorocarbon is nearly invisible underwater and ideal for clear water. Braided line offers maximum strength and sensitivity, preferred for heavy cover and saltwater.

Does Bass Pro Shops offer free shipping on fishing gear?

What is the best lure for bass fishing?

Top choices include soft plastic worms, spinnerbaits, crankbaits, and bass jigs. Topwater lures like poppers and frogs are exciting in warmer months when bass are feeding near the surface.
